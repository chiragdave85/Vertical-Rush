HOW TO PLAY

  1. Time your drop. Tap the arena, use Place block, or press Space when the moving block aligns with the tower.
  2. Stay precise. Overhanging edges fall away. Perfect drops keep your block whole and build a melodic streak.
  3. Keep climbing. Classic uses a steady, flowing pace. In Zen, movement is slower and a miss gives you another try.

P or ESC pauses and resumes. Switching tabs pauses automatically. Opening settings pauses an active run; close settings and resume when you’re ready.
